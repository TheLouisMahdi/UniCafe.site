# Coffee image refresh — 2026-10-28

Built-in image_gen was used with the owner's attached poster as a visual reference. These are decorative generated product images, not photographs of the actual vending output. Existing machine photographs and menu data are unchanged.

Final public WebP assets are in `assets/`: `cup-teal-v2.webp`, `robusta-v2.webp`, `arabica-v2.webp`, `hazelnut-v2.webp`, `decaf-v2.webp`, plus their `thumb-v2` versions. Large images are 768 px wide; menu thumbnails are 192 px wide. Alpha is retained for the teal cup. Full-resolution PNG masters are retained locally in ignored `artifacts/cup-masters/`; they are not shipped to the website.
